Utilities

Small browser-based helpers we built for our experiments. Everything runs locally in your browser — no data is uploaded.

Bpod State Matrix Parser

Parses and analyzes Bpod 2+ state matrix data. Supports tuple and binary input and gives a detailed breakdown of states, timers, counters and conditions.

Open tool →

Coordinate Angle Calculator

Calculates new stereotaxic coordinates after tilting the arm or the sample by a given angle, with an interactive plot of the transformation.

Open tool →

Plate Layout Generator

Turns an Excel plate sheet into a printable layout. Reads well labels and cell colours straight from the .xlsx and lays them out as a one-page A4 PDF, for any plate from 6 to 384 wells.

Open tool →

Noise Generator

Generates customizable noise across multiple frequency bands, with presets, precise volume control and a timer — for sleep and focus.
